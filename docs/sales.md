# Sales

Sales converts qualified interest into an informed mutual commitment. It owns
qualification after substantive interest, discovery preparation,
decision-criteria mapping, evidence-backed positioning, interview and pitch
preparation, objections, next-step design, proposal and negotiation support
within explicit constraints, conversion follow-up, loss analysis, and sales
artifact delivery.

Outreach owns target research, first touch, and response classification.
Content Manager owns reusable demos, case studies, public writing, and media.
Portfolio Strategist owns priorities and market selection. Director owns
execution coordination. Sales does not send, publish, promise terms, accept
offers, or make external commitments without explicit authority.

The deterministic evaluation pack rejects unsupported claims, coercive
conversion, poor-fit optimization, and unauthorized commitments. Frontier
evidence is required before any lower-tier lane can be enabled.

See [evaluation matrices](evaluation-matrices.md), [model tiers](model-tiers.md),
and [role briefings](role-briefings.md).
