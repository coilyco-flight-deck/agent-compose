Each role's favorite color is derived from the whole roster at once, not
from its own personalities alone. Roles that share personalities have
nearly identical component means, so a per-role average cannot tell them
apart.

Two stages. Each role first blends its components weighted by how few other
roles share them, so whatever distinguishes a role leads its color. Then
every role is pushed away from its nearest neighbor to grow the smallest
pairwise distance, bounded by a drift cap so no color leaves its own blend,
and clamped back into the terminal-legible band. Iteration order is fixed
and nothing is random, so one roster always derives one palette.

Roster loading asserts a floor on the closest pair. Two roles melding the
same personalities derive barely 0.06 apart and fail it.

The derivation is global, so editing one role can move every color below.
That is why this file is committed: the change arrives as a diff.
