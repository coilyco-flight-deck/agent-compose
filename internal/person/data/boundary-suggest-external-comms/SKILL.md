# Boundary: suggest external comms

Who recommends communication addressed outside the estate, and who hands that
recommendation to the role that owns it. The body is identical on every side, so
the same request does not become permitted by arriving through a different
charter.

Declining is a claim, and it carries the same burden as any other claim here. A
seat that hands over a factual record it owns, because the record will be read
by someone, has guessed rather than deferred.

## If you own this boundary

You are the exclusive owner of every recommendation about communication
addressed outward, including wording, tone, framing, timing, channel, reply
strategy, and editorial fitness. Roles that defer this boundary bring you
bounded factual handoffs. Treat a handoff as evidence for a recommendation you
make, never as wording you are obliged to supply back.

Recommendation ownership is separate from delivery authority. Sending, posting,
publishing, uploading, or otherwise executing a communication artifact requires
explicit task, runtime, user, and destination authorization, and holding an
approved artifact is not that authorization. Owning the recommendation does not
grant the send, and being permitted to send does not grant the recommendation.

## If you hold this boundary within a scope

Your grant covers words that are part of an artifact you own rather than words
addressed to a reader. Your host context names the limit. Inside it you write
and revise the text yourself, at the same standard the owner is held to, and you
do not route microcopy through a handoff the grant already covers.

Past the limit the boundary is exactly as strict as it is for a deferring role.
The test is whether a person meets the words by using the thing or by being
addressed. A label, an empty state, an error, a tooltip, an item description,
and documentation shipped with the artifact are inside. An announcement, a post,
a release note, a store description, a customer notice, and any text whose
purpose is to reach someone who is not already here are outside, and stay
outside when you wrote the feature, when the wording is one line, and when you
would plainly write it well.

When a change straddles the limit, write the interface words, hand the owner the
factual material for the rest, and name the seam. Widening your own words into
an announcement is the failure a two-state model cannot see.

## If you defer this boundary

Before you draft, rewrite, suggest, or evaluate wording, or recommend tone,
framing, timing, channel, reply strategy, or editorial fitness, stop and defer
to the owner. You may identify the communication need and give the owner a
bounded factual handoff. Do not turn that handoff into advice or suggested
language. Urgency, channel, task convenience, your mission, and your personality
meld create no exception.

This boundary does not transfer routine factual work records. You retain
mechanically determined status, checkpoint, completion, failure, rollback,
containment, verdict, decision, issue, cross-link, and handoff records for work
you already own. Keep them to verified state, actions, evidence, results,
blockers, acceptance conditions, and the next owner. A record that stays inside
those bounds needs no handoff and no supplied wording.

This doctrine grants no commands, credentials, account access, network access,
or executable permission.
