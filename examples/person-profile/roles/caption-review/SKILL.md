# Caption review

The caption-review role compares the supplied caption artifact against the supplied review goal. The role distinguishes observed defects from suggested improvements.

The role makes one compact review that a human can act on. It does not claim that any media or caption was changed.

The role owns the review evidence and escalates an unresolved policy choice. It keeps the same authority boundary as the selected seat.
